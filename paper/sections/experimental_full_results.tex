On the archived Burgers PINN, GrassRoute reduces best training loss relative
to both AdamW and tuned periodic SVD while using 84.1\% less optimizer
state. This is one PDE task, with a reported 1.78$\times$ step cost. The
metric combines residual and initial-condition errors rather than measuring
relative solution error. The omitted boundary penalty also limits physical
interpretation (Appendix~\ref{app:audit}).

On Cora, GrassRoute improves on AdamW but falls slightly below tuned
periodic SVD. In the archived CNN run, partial routing improves accuracy by
1.96 percentage points over AdamW and 0.19 points over tuned SGD. Its
88.19 MB state, compared with AdamW's 89.76 MB, saves about 1.8\%.
Full routing uses 8.68 MB but loses 11.91 accuracy points to AdamW.
The two variants therefore offer different accuracy--memory trade-offs.
The graph-arxiv, MLP, recommendation, and continual-learning results show
no robust GrassRoute advantage. The ViT archive contains complete AdamW
and periodic-SVD histories but no complete GrassRoute result.

The archived diagnostics cannot establish a single geometric predictor of
performance. Captured energy does not measure an eigengap, consecutive-basis
angles do not measure oracle error, and nominal rank sweeps did not always
change parameter-group ranks. The controlled diagnostics address these
measurement issues while retaining the original outcomes.

The original manifest covers each proposed task and dataset with a
representative model, rather than every dataset--architecture--rank
combination. Its 27 configurations comprise three input dimensions each
for matrix and low-rank regression, MNIST, four CNN datasets, three ViT
datasets, four graph datasets, four scientific tasks, three recommendation
datasets, and two continual-learning protocols. Each method screens three
learning rates on validation data before a fresh final run. Completed runs
use full epochs and save every epoch, restartable checkpoints, dataset
sources, actual ranks, hyperparameters, and code hashes. The expensive queue
was stopped after 117 completed cells; the others remain unfinished.
Appendix~\ref{app:audit} defines the tasks and search budgets.

\input{figures/scientific_endpoints_table}

Only completed comparisons support a task-level conclusion. Memory, test
quality, and time are reported separately, including the strong SGD and
fixed-routing results. The finite search and single-seed protocol do not
establish statistical significance or an exhaustive optimizer ranking.

\subsection{Focused exploratory follow-up after scope reduction}
\label{app:focused-followup}
After the original 162-cell plan stopped at 117 completed runs, remaining
cells, including partial CIFAR-100 histories, were marked unfinished.
A smaller follow-up tests tracking on a rotating rank-four regression stream
under three conditions, fixed before the final results were examined.
Each method receives four equal-budget validation candidates and a fresh
1500-step run. Table~\ref{tab:focused-regression} reports every condition
and method. GrassRoute tracks the subspace more closely under drift but
does not achieve lower prediction MSE than the strongest baselines.
Motivated by the Cora GraphSAGE result, a separate GCN experiment tests
another architecture on the same dataset. This exploratory replication's
six-method comparison is in Table~\ref{tab:focused-gcn}.
